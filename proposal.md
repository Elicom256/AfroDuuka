# 🔄 The Order Workflow: My suggestion
1. Quote: The wholesaler creates a price offer. Inventory is not affected, and no sale is recorded.
2. Sales Order (SO): The client accepts the quote. The system creates a Sales Order. Inventory is now "Allocated" or "Reserved" so no one else can buy it, but it is still physically in the warehouse. Still, no financial sale is recorded yet.
3. Shipment / Delivery: The wholesaler picks, packs, and ships the goods. Physical inventory drops.
4. Invoice: The wholesaler bills the client. This is the exact moment the sale is officially recorded in the system. Revenue is recognized, and accounts receivable goes up.
🛑 Why recording a sale too early causes problems
• Ruins Inventory Accuracy: If the client rejects the quote, your system will incorrectly show that stock has left the warehouse, causing you to turn away actual buyers.
• Distorts Financial Reports: Your profit and loss statements will show "ghost revenue" from deals that might never happen, which can lead to major tax and accounting errors.
